**This file is the acceptance criterion.** Every brief for this surface must cite the items it
satisfies, and every change must be checked against the WHOLE list, not the newest message.

Why it exists: three separate requirements were built narrower than stated, all by the same
mechanism — the owner's words were translated into the codebase's existing vocabulary before
being passed on, and the translation lost the requirement. `datasets` became `axes, filtered`;
`name them PCA/UMAP` became `break ties when contested`; `group by dataset` became `group, but
suppress the heading`. Nothing in the gate pipeline compares a result to the owner's words, so a
translation error is invisible until they look at the screen.
